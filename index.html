Official Party Guide

How to Play Grab The Mic 🎤

1. The Table Mic Setup & 50-Point Race

Place one real microphone in the exact center of the table. First team to reach 50 points (or the team with the highest score when End Game is clicked) wins!

2. The Scramble & Sing Timer

The instant a word appears, sprint/reach for the mic. The host taps MIC GRABBED! (or hits Enter). The mic holder must start singing a real song containing that word in the title or lyrics before the timer buzzes.

+1 PT
Lyric Only

Typed/sung lyric contains the prompt word.

+2 PTS
Song Only

Real song whose title or lyrics contain the word.

+3 PTS
Artist & Song

Named BOTH the artist and the song title!

-1 PT
Freeze / Bluff

Froze, repeated a song, or faked the lyric.

3. Chaos Twists & Host Challenge

  • ⚡ Lightning Round: Timer drops to 4 seconds—pure reflex!
  • 🔄 Pass-The-Mic Chain: After singing, gently pass the mic to another player who must sing a different song with the same word!
  • 🕵️ Host Challenge: Anyone can shout "Check!" if they think the word isn't really in the song. Wrong singer loses 1 pt; wrong challenger loses 1 pt.
